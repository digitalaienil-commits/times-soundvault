# Content ID readiness

## Asset, reference and video

- A YouTube asset is the Content Manager representation of intellectual
  property and its ownership/policy metadata.
- A reference is the individual eligible audio or video source used for Content
  ID matching. It is not a public YouTube video.
- A video is an uploaded audiovisual item. SoundVault's concatenated test video
  is operational evidence only and is never a reference.

YouTube requires exclusive rights for reference material. Non-exclusive
licensed material is not approved for future registration. Production music,
generic soundbeds, loops, sound effects, ambience, compilations, third-party
samples and insufficiently distinct recordings need cautious human policy
review. Owned or exclusively licensed declarations are only potentially
eligible until a Coordinator/Admin completes the checklist; declarations are
not verified rights or legal advice.

Readiness is separate from eligibility. Missing title, artist/creator, rights
owner/label, ISRC where applicable, territory, period or rights basis results in
`needs_metadata` or a rights/policy review state. SoundVault never fabricates
these fields and Section 6 creates no reference.
